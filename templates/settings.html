How limits are combined

  • Per downloader: an active time rule replaces the default. If several rules match, the lowest wins.
  • While streaming: the budget share is applied on top — whichever is lower wins. A stream never weakens a stricter rule.
  • A rule with mode "Pause" stops that downloader completely and removes it from the budget split.
  • JDownloader's pause keeps connections alive at minimal speed, so resuming is instant.
